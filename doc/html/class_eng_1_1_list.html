Detailed Description

Flat list of the lights and meshes of a scene graph. The lights are drawn before the meshes, so that every mesh is lit by all of them. The list does not own the nodes.

◆ renderShadow()

void ENG_API Eng::List::renderShadow ( const glm::mat4 & viewMatrix,
Light * light )

Renders the planar shadow cast by a light on the floor. The objects are flattened on the floor with the shadow matrix of the OpenGL SuperBible (MakeShadowMatrix, chapter 9): M = (plane . light) I - light plane^T, drawn only into the stencil buffer. The floor is then drawn again, without the light, where the stencil marks the shadow. Only meshes lower than a point light cast a shadow: a point above the light cannot be projected on the floor.

Parameters
viewMatrixview matrix of the camera
lightlight that casts the shadow (nothing happens if nullptr or off)
